# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

River and anyone handed the working paper under time pressure (research, coaching debate, analysis audit). Context: desk reading, daylight or cool lamp, high stakes on getting the estimand right. Job: extract the quantity, the decision rule, and what was *not* measured, without marketing fluff.

## Product Purpose

Long-form empirical research surfaces for League of Legends analytics (starting with void-grub intrinsic value). Success: a reader can state the estimand, cite preferred Δobj in pp, and apply the contest-vs-leave rule without confusing take-regime association for package price.

## Brand Personality

clinical, statistical, cold

Voice is spare, precise, and adversarial toward conflated estimands. Emotion: composure and rigor, not enthusiasm. No coach-speak, no “insights,” no celebration of findings.

## Anti-references

- SaaS hero-metric cards (big number, small label, pastel chips)
- Purple / indigo gradient tech landing pages
- Cream background + display serif “editorial AI” magazine layouts
- Broadsheet newspaper pastiche (hairline rules, dense columns as costume)
- Emoji callouts, motivational coaching tone, “key takeaways” blog structure
- Dark neon dashboards sold as “analytics”

## Design Principles

1. **Name the estimand first.** Every surface states what is measured and what is not.
2. **Numbers over narrative.** Prefer tables and figures; prose exists to define and bound them.
3. **Two-decimal discipline.** Display map-WR effects in pp to two decimals; full precision stays in JSON.
4. **Cold clarity.** No warmth performance, no hype; leave defaults and avoid contests when the math says so.
5. **Anti-conflation.** Never present take-regime association as mechanical package price.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Screen- and print-first. Body measure about 65-75ch equivalent in PDF. Tables carry chart meaning (not color alone). High contrast ink on paper-tinted ground. No motion required for comprehension.
