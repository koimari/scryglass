# Scryglass

## Register
product

## Product Purpose
Fast League of Legends rankings for teams, players, and champions. The public site uses small accepted data files and refreshes them every six hours.

## Users
- Fans checking current team ratings
- Fans comparing player ratings
- Analysts checking patch-wide champion tier lists

## Brand personality
Credible editorial × Kyle Anthony Miller tactical industrial. Warm sand / warm charcoal. Safety orange + field olive. Airy hierarchy with dense micro-labels. Not hospital, not empty Notion, not financial-dashboard card grids.

## Anti-references
- Clinical cool whites / consultório blues
- Empty minimalist zero-info heroes
- Purple SaaS / glassmorphism / parchment broadsheet
- Betting tip-sheet / odds-board aesthetics

## Positioning
Scryglass is a lightweight League of Legends rankings site by koi.

## Known traps
- Publishing a partial refresh
- Accepting malformed game identities
- Publishing league-specific tier boards
